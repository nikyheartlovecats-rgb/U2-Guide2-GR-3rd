Unit 5 Grammar Reference - Our World 3

Welcome to your digital board! Review the key grammatical structures for Unit 5: Asking questions with Why...? / Because... and expressing purpose using Infinitives of Purpose (to + verb).

Grammar 1: Asking "Why...?" and Answering with "Because..."

We use Why to ask about reasons, and we answer with Because to explain the reason why something happens or why animals/people behave in a certain way.

Question Word Auxiliary / Verb Subject Main Verb / Complement Answer Starter Subject Reason / Explanation
Why do snakes come out during the day? Because they like the sun.
don't you like crocodiles? they are scary.
can frogs jump high? they have strong legs.
can't birds climb trees? they don't have arms.

Grammar 2: Infinitive of Purpose (to + verb)

We use an infinitive of purpose (to + base verb) to explain why someone uses something or why animals/people perform an action.

Subject Verb Possessive / Object Body Part / Tool Infinitive of Purpose Additional Detail
Parrots use their wings to fly.
Cats use their tongues to clean their fur.
Kangaroos use their pouches to carry their babies.

Resolved Examples (15 Total)

Study these carefully structured examples across Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced levels.

Basic Level (1 to 5)

Example 1: Why question (Do)

Question: Why do fish swim in the ocean?

Answer: Because they live in water.

Explanation: Uses present simple auxiliary "do" for general facts about animals.

Example 2: Why question (Don't)

Question: Why don't monkeys live in the snow?

Answer: Because they like warm climates.

Explanation: Uses negative auxiliary "don't" to ask about animal habits.

Example 3: Why question (Can)

Question: Why can monkeys climb trees?

Answer: Because they have long arms and tails.

Explanation: Uses modal verb "can" inquiring about animal physical ability.

Example 4: Infinitive of Purpose (Teeth)

Sentence: Sharks use their sharp teeth to eat fish.

Explanation: Connects body part (teeth) directly with purpose using "to + eat".

Example 5: Infinitive of Purpose (Wings)

Sentence: Eagles use their wings to fly high in the sky.

Explanation: Identifies bird anatomy and its primary function.

Intermediate Level (6 to 10)

Example 6: Habitat and Environment Query

Question: Why is the rabbit going underground?

Answer: Because its home is underground.

Explanation: Combines "Why is" (present continuous context) with possessive pronoun "its".

Example 7: Behavior and Climate

Question: Why are the elephants in the water?

Answer: Because the sun is hot, and elephants feel cool in the water.

Explanation: Multi-clause explanation combining environmental temperature and animal comfort.

Example 8: Infinitive with Complex Object

Sentence: Polar bears use their thick white fur to keep warm in the ice and snow.

Explanation: Utilizes a two-word infinitive phrase "to keep warm" with environmental context.

Example 9: Modal Can't Query

Question: Why can't penguins fly in the sky?

Answer: Because their wings are adapted to swim in the ocean.

Explanation: Explores negative modal capability ("can't") with specialized adaptation.

Example 10: Infinitive for Protection

Sentence: Turtles use their hard shells to protect themselves from predators.

Explanation: Links anatomy to defensive purpose using reflexive pronouns.

Advanced Level (11 to 15)

Example 11: Multi-conditional Habitat Interaction

Question: Why do zebras run when they see lions in the grasslands?

Answer: Because they are scared, and they need to escape danger.

Explanation: Combines "Why do", dependent time/condition clauses ("when they see"), and multiple reasons.

Example 12: Complex Infinitive of Purpose (Giraffes)

Sentence: Giraffes use their long necks and long tongues to reach leaves high up in acacia trees.

Explanation: Combines two body parts with an expanded infinitive phrase describing precise ecological behavior.

Example 13: Comparative Behavior Query

Question: Why do nocturnal animals hunt at night instead of during the day?

Answer: Because it is cooler and helps them avoid daytime predators.

Explanation: Integrates advanced ecological vocabulary ("nocturnal", "predators") within a comparative framework.

Example 14: Specialized Anatomical Purpose

Sentence: Chameleons use their color-changing skin to camouflage themselves and communicate with other chameleons.

Explanation: Features dual compound purposes joined by "and" following the infinitive marker "to".

Example 15: Complex Ecosystem Interdependence

Question: Why are birds important in wetlands like swamps and marshes?

Answer: Because they control insect populations, and their movements help maintain ecosystem balance.

Explanation: Synthesizes scientific inquiry ("Why are") with systemic environmental consequence.

Practice Exercises (15 Total with Step-by-Step Solutions)

Basic Level Exercises (1 to 5)

Exercise 1 (Why / Because)

Complete: ______ fish run? — ______ they don't have legs.

Step-1: Identify the subject ("fish") and the action ("run").

Step-2: Determine the auxiliary for fish (plural noun = don't) and start question with "Why".

Step-3: Answer reason using "Because".

Correct Answer: Why don't fish run? — Because they don't have legs.

Exercise 2 (Why / Because)

Complete: ______ hippos stand in water? — ______ the sun is hot, and hippos feel cool.

Step-1: Check subject ("hippos", plural) and verb ("stand").

Step-2: Use auxiliary "do" for the question.

Step-3: Answer with "Because".

Correct Answer: Do hippos stand in water? — Because the sun is hot...

Exercise 3 (Infinitive of Purpose)

Complete: Goats use their horns ______.

Step-1: Recall what goats do with horns (defend / fight).

Step-2: Form infinitive using "to + verb" (to fight).

Correct Answer: Goats use their horns to fight.

Exercise 4 (Infinitive of Purpose)

Complete: Lions use their teeth ______ meat.

Step-1: Identify the tool ("teeth") and action with meat ("eat").

Step-2: Apply infinitive structure "to eat".

Correct Answer: Lions use their teeth to eat meat.

Exercise 5 (Why question form)

Complete: ______ the desert dry? — ______ there is very little rain.

Step-1: The main verb is "is" (to be), not an action verb.

Step-2: Form question with "Why is".

Correct Answer: Why is the desert dry? — Because there is very little rain.

Intermediate Level Exercises (6 to 10)

Exercise 6 (Why / Because Matching)

Match & Complete: Why are the zebras running? — Match with: [ Because they see a lion, and they are scared. ]

Step-1: Analyze present continuous question ("are... running").

Step-2: Connect flight behavior with predator stimulus (lion).

Correct Answer: Because they see a lion, and they are scared.

Exercise 7 (Infinitive: Polar Bears)

Complete: Polar bears use their white fur ______ in the ice and snow.

Step-1: Think about camouflage and cold weather.

Step-2: Use "to hide" or "to keep warm".

Correct Answer: ...use their white fur to hide / to keep warm in the ice and snow.

Exercise 8 (Why question: Birds in Wetlands)

Complete: ______ the birds in the wetlands? — ______ they eat frogs and fish.

Step-1: Subject is plural ("the birds"), verb is prepositional location.

Step-2: Form question: "Why are the birds in the wetlands?"

Correct Answer: Why are the birds in the wetlands? — Because they eat frogs and fish.

Exercise 9 (Infinitive: Giraffes)

Complete: Giraffes use their long tongues ______.

Step-1: What do giraffes reach with tongues? Leaves on trees.

Step-2: Form infinitive: "to reach" or "to eat".

Correct Answer: Giraffes use their long tongues to reach leaves.

Exercise 10 (Why question: Tiger)

Complete: ______ the tiger drink water? — ______ it is thirsty.

Step-1: Subject is singular ("the tiger"). Action verb is "drink".

Step-2: Use auxiliary "does".

Correct Answer: Does the tiger drink water? — Because it is thirsty.

Advanced Level Exercises (11 to 15)

Exercise 11 (Complex Purpose Formulation)

Create & Complete: Write a sentence explaining why owls use their large wings and silent feathers.

Step-1: Identify owl traits (wings/feathers) and nocturnal hunting style.

Step-2: Combine using subject + use + object + multiple infinitives.

Correct Answer: Owls use their wings and silent feathers to fly quietly and catch prey at night.

Exercise 12 (Multi-clause Why / Because)

Complete & Expand: Why do desert animals burrow underground during scorching hot days?

Step-1: Address temperature and moisture preservation.

Step-2: Formulate complete explanatory response.

Correct Answer: Because it protects them from extreme heat and helps them stay cool.

Exercise 13 (Infinitive of Purpose Transformation)

Transform: Rewrite using an infinitive of purpose: "Kangaroos have pouches so that they can carry their babies." -> "Kangaroos use their pouches ______."

Step-1: Locate core action ("carry their babies").

Step-2: Convert clause to infinitive phrase ("to carry their babies").

Correct Answer: Kangaroos use their pouches to carry their babies.

Exercise 14 (Critical Habitat Query)

Formulate: Create a "Why... / Because..." question and answer pair about penguins living in ice habitats.

Step-1: Use modal or present simple for penguins ("Why do penguins live in ice habitats?").

Step-2: Formulate ecological response ("Because they have thick blubber and feathers to keep warm").

Correct Answer: Why do penguins live in ice? — Because they have blubber to keep warm.

Exercise 15 (Advanced Synthesis)

Complete: Why do chameleons change their skin color, and what do they use it for?

Step-1: Address dual biological function (camouflage + communication).

Step-2: Combine "Because..." with infinitive phrase.

Correct Answer: Because they need survival mechanisms; they use it to hide and communicate.

Critical Reflection Questions

Think deeply about these open questions and discuss them with your partner or teacher.

Reflection 1: Animal Adaptations

Why do you think different animals in different habitats (like deserts, rainforests, and icy regions) have such different body parts? How do infinitives of purpose help us describe these survival tools?

Reflection 2: Cause and Effect

When asking "Why...?" about nature, why is understanding the "Because..." explanation important for protecting wildlife and ecosystems?

Reflection 3: Personal Connection

Think of an animal you love (for example, a dolphin, a hawk, or a wolf). What is one special tool or body part it uses every day, and what is its purpose?
